    How to play
    • Pick an upgrade. Each guess tells you how it compares to the secret one.
    • Green means it matches. Red means it doesn't.
    • Tier and Cost show an arrow pointing toward the answer: 🔼 means the answer is higher.
    • Type is the tower's category: Primary, Military, Magic or Support.
    • Paragons are in the pool as tier 6 with their own path. Costs are Medium difficulty. You get 6 guesses.
    • A new upgrade every day (UTC). Data from bloonswiki.com.
